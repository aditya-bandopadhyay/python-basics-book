# D3: An algorithm for a bowl of instant noodles

1. Take one packet of instant noodles and one saucepan from the shelf.
2. Fill a measuring cup with 300 ml of tap water and pour it into the saucepan.
3. Place the saucepan on the stove and turn the burner to high.
4. Look at the water. If it is not bubbling all over, wait 30 seconds and repeat this step.
5. Tear open the noodle packet along the notch. Take out the small seasoning sachet and put it aside.
6. Put the noodle block into the boiling water.
7. Tear open the seasoning sachet and empty it into the water.
8. Stir with a spoon and set a timer for 2 minutes.
9. When the timer rings, turn the burner off.
10. Pour the noodles and liquid into a bowl. Stop.

**Checking the three rules**

- *Finite*: there are 10 steps; the only repeated step (4) ends because heated water eventually boils.
- *Definite*: every step names exact objects, amounts, and times ("300 ml", "2 minutes"), so there is only one way to read it.
- *Effective*: every step is a simple physical action a person (or a robot) can carry out.
